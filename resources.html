Nursing by numbers

The register in plain figures, straight from the NMC.

867,935 on the register · NMC→
A record high — around 2.5% of the UK working population. 41,542 joined this year against 30,323 who left.
Free
Growth has nearly halved · NMC→
The register grew 1.7% between April 2025 and March 2026, against 3.3% the year before.
Free
International recruitment is falling · NMC→
Internationally-educated professionals were 26.4% of new joiners this year, down from 39.1% the year before and a high of 49.4% in 2023–24. UK-educated joiners also fell, by 4.9%.
Free

Figures verified against the NMC's own registration statistics page on 28/08/2026. The NMC publishes a fuller breakdown by field of practice and nation in its linked reports — worth a look if you want the detail behind these headlines.

For registered nurses

Working for yourself

Self-employed or independent practice — two compliance traps worth knowing before you start.

Indemnity covers your actual practice · NMC→
The Code requires an indemnity arrangement that provides appropriate cover for whatever practice you take on. It's self-declared at registration and renewal, and it's each registrant's own responsibility to check the cover fits the work — an employer's policy won't automatically extend to work done outside that employment.
Free
Ear wax removal needs CQC registration · RCN→
The RCN's own self-employment guide names this as the example that catches people out: it's a regulated activity requiring CQC registration when carried out by a listed healthcare professional, including registered nurses. Doing it unregistered is a criminal offence.
Free
The full self-employment guide · RCN→
Tax, insurance, contracts and what counts as a "regulated activity" for other independent nursing work.
Free

Verified against the RCN and NMC's own pages, 28/08/2026. This isn't a substitute for checking your own specific scope of practice against the CQC's regulated activities list before you start.

For registered nurses

What you can claim

Tax relief most nurses are owed and don't claim — figures checked against HMRC's own pages, not a rebate company.

NMC fee & RCN membership are deductible · HMRC→
Both the NMC and RCN are on HMRC's own approved list, so their fees qualify for tax relief when you pay them yourself. You can claim for the current tax year and the 4 previous tax years.
Free
£125 a year for uniform & laundering · HMRC→
HMRC's flat-rate expense for nurses, midwives, healthcare assistants and other therapists — covers laundering your own uniform and includes the shoes/tights allowance where a specific colour or style is required. Ambulance staff on active service have a separate, higher rate (£185).
Free
How to claim · HMRC→
Send copies of receipts or other evidence of what you paid. If you complete Self Assessment, claim through your tax return instead of the online service.
Free

Checked against gov.uk's own pages on 28/08/2026 — not a tax-rebate company's figures. HMRC's published flat rate for this group is £125, not the higher ambulance-staff rate of £185 that's sometimes quoted for nurses in error.
