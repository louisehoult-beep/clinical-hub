The essentials

Registration & must-dos

The things that keep you working, checked against the official sources.

NMC revalidation — every three years: 450 practice hours, 35 hours of continuing professional development (20 participatory), five pieces of practice-related feedback, five written reflective accounts, a reflective discussion, health and character, indemnity, and a confirmation.
The Code — the NMC's four themes: prioritise people, practise effectively, preserve safety, promote professionalism and trust. Every reflective account is written against one of them.
Care Certificate — now 16 standards, after learning disability and autism were added in 2025. The CQC-expected baseline for everyone new to care, and the thing a nursing associate route builds on.
Disclosure and Barring Service (DBS) — clinical and care roles need an Enhanced check with a barred-list check. The Update Service costs £16 a year and makes it portable between employers.
Your nation matters — Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland require personal registration for social care workers (Scottish Social Services Council, Social Care Wales, Northern Ireland Social Care Council). England does not.
